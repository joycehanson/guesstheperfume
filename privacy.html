Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version: the game is free and needs no sign up. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no session recordings. We only store the player name you choose, your scores and any result you decide to share, so the leaderboard and share links can work. We never sell your data.

What we store and why

Your player name

The name you type in before playing. It appears publicly on the leaderboard and on any result you share. You can pick any nickname, and we suggest you do not use your full real name.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, providing the game you asked to play.

An anonymous player account

When you first play, the game creates an anonymous account with a random ID, so your scores stay linked to you. It does not ask for an email address, a password or any other contact details.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Your scores

For each finished game we store your player name, score, game mode and the time. The best scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Results you share

When you tap to share a result, we save the result picture and a short record (player name, score, game mode, rank and how many bottles you got right) under a random link. This lets the link show a preview picture wherever you post it. Anyone with the link can see it. Nothing is saved unless you choose to share.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Technical server logs

Like every website, our hosting providers briefly process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request. This is needed to deliver the game and keep it secure. We do not use it to build profiles.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in running the game securely.

Saved on your device

The game keeps a few items in your browser's local storage: your player name, the anonymous sign in key, your recent scores, which bottles you have already seen (so rounds do not repeat) and similar game settings. These are needed for the game to work, so no consent banner is required (Section 25(2) TDDDG). We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Services we use

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA) runs the database, the anonymous accounts and the storage for shared result pictures. The game's data is stored in Supabase's Frankfurt, Germany region.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., USA) hosts the game and the share links, and processes the technical server logs described above.

Both work for us as processors under data processing terms. Where data may be accessed from outside the EU, it is protected by the safeguards in those terms, such as the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. The game's fonts and images are served from our own site, so no data goes to Google Fonts or similar services.

If you tap a share button for X, WhatsApp or another app, that app opens and its own privacy policy applies to what you post there.

How long we keep it

  • Leaderboard entries and shared results: for as long as the game is online, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Server logs: only for a short time, as set by our hosting providers.
  • Data on your device: until you clear your browser data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). To use any of these rights, for example to remove a leaderboard entry or a shared result, email hello@studiohans.agency and tell us the player name and roughly when you played.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. Ours is Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, but you can contact the authority in your own country too.

Changes

If the game changes how it uses data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
